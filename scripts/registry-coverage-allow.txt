# spec-bearing guest routine deliberately has NO row in either proof registry
# (Progress/Routines.lean or Progress/Correspondence.lean) — and says why.

# or leaves the guest image, the run fails with STALE and names the line to delete.
# That is the point: an exemption that outlives its reason is how a backlog goes
# silent again, which is the defect #11637 recorded (~103 linked, proven routines
# invisible to every census, so the proven-count understated reality).
